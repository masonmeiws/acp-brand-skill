# Asu Capital Partners and AG1 brand

Use this skill for any material that carries the ACP or AG1 name: decks, documents, memos, LP reports, charts, web pages, formatted emails and spreadsheets with charts. Posters are made by hand. For posters, apply only the logo, type and colour rules.

Files in this skill:
- `assets/logos/`: the official logo files. Use them as they are.
- `references/brand-guidelines.pdf`: the full guidelines with examples. Open it when you need to see an example.

## Procedure

1. Decide the brand: ACP, AG1 or both. Decide the output: a brand piece (cover, title page, web page) or an information piece (body text, tables, charts).
2. Take the logo from `assets/logos/`. Never redraw, trace or approximate the logo. If you cannot place an image, write "Asu Capital Partners" in text.
3. Apply the rules below.
4. Build the output:
   - Deck or slides: titles in Lora, body in Google Sans, white ground.
   - Document or report: titles in Lora, body in Google Sans (Noto Sans JP or Noto Sans SC for Japanese and Chinese), white ground.
   - Chart: follow the chart template below.
   - Web page: use the colour and type values below.
5. Check the result against the final check. Render it and look at it before you deliver it.

## Brand

- ACP is the firm. ACP invests and operates AG1. ACP is the first sun of tomorrow: clear, calm and a little cool. ACP type is open (positive letter-spacing).
- AG1 is a 10-week live-in residency in Tokyo for global founders. AG1 is a home for founders: warm and close, a community first. AG1 type is tight (negative letter-spacing). AG1 uses warm grounds and real photographs of people together.
- AG1 has its own name and voice. ACP stands behind it: "by Asu Capital Partners".
- Write in short, plain sentences. No hype words, no exclamation marks, no emoji.

AG1, English: AG1 is a 10-week live-in residency in central Tokyo for founders from anywhere in the world. Founders live and build together and work with the ACP team every week. The program ends with Demo Day. AG1 is free and takes no equity. Asu Capital Partners operates AG1.

## Logo

Files in `assets/logos/`:
- `acp-logo-gradient-stacked.png`: the default logo. White and light grounds.
- `acp-logo-slate.svg` (#304048): general use where colour would distract: website headers, documents, chart footers, rows of investor logos.
- `acp-logo-white.svg`: solid colour grounds and calm areas of photographs.
- `acp-mark-gradient.png`, `acp-mark-slate.svg`, `acp-mark-white.svg`: the mark alone. It can stand alone: avatars, merch, small square spaces.
- `asu-kanji-lockup-color.png`: 明日資本. Japanese formal use: Japanese LP materials, corporate partners.

Rules:
- Keep clear space on all sides equal to half the logo's height.
- Minimum size: logo 64px wide on screen and 16mm in print; mark 24px and 8mm. Below that, write "Asu Capital Partners" in text.
- A short phrase next to the logo, such as "Presented by", is allowed.
- Do not stretch, rotate, crop, recolour, add shadows or effects, or put the logo on a busy image.
- Do not build a new lockup from the mark and typed text.

## AG1 mark

- AG1 has no logo file. Set "AG1" in Lora Medium (500), letter-spacing -0.03em.
- Ink #1A1A1A on light grounds, white on dark grounds.
- Endorse in Lora: "by Asu Capital Partners".
- Do not add a dot, a box, a seal, a colour or a second typeface. Do not use Helvetica for AG1.

## Partnerships

- Put the two logos side by side at the same visual height, with "×" between them.
- Ask the partner for a logo on a transparent background.
- Match our version to theirs: colour partner logo, use our gradient logo; one-colour partner logo, use our slate logo; on solid colour or photographs, both logos white.
- Never recolour the partner logo. Both sides approve each co-branded piece.

## Typography

| Use | English | 日本語 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Titles, names, quotes | Lora | Zen Old Mincho | Noto Serif SC |
| Body, tables, charts, UI | Google Sans | Noto Sans JP | Noto Sans SC |

- All are free on Google Fonts. Fallbacks: Lora to Georgia; Google Sans to Arial or system-ui.
- Weights 400 and 500. Poster titles may use 600.
- ACP display: Lora 400, +0.03em. ACP section titles: Lora 500, +0.02em.
- AG1 display: Lora 500, -0.03em. AG1 section titles: Lora 500, -0.015em.
- Italic for one line per page only (a tagline or a pull quote).
- Sentence case for titles and buttons. Japanese and Chinese body: line height 1.75.
- Never mix a serif body and a sans body on one page.

## Elements

- Separate sections with space. Use thin lines only inside lists. No boxes around everything.
- Corners: 8px for ACP, 10px for AG1, about 32px on photographs in 1080px posters. Pills for buttons. No resting shadows.
- Photographs: real people, the residence, Tokyo. No stock, no illustrations, no AI images.

## Chart template

Anatomy:
- Title: Lora 500, 22px. Write the finding ("Deal flow doubled in a year"), not the topic.
- Subtitle: Google Sans 13px, #5F5A55. Give the measure, the unit and the period.
- Plot: Google Sans 12px, tabular figures, axis text #5F5A55. Horizontal hairline grid only (#1A1A1A at 8%). No vertical grid, no chart border, no 3D, no pie charts.
- Lines 2px with a 9px end dot (2px white ring). Bars at most 24px thick, 4px rounded data end, square at the baseline. 2px white gap between touching bars or stacked segments.
- Labels: the last value or the one value that matters. Never a number on every point. A legend for two or more series; none for one series.
- Footer: "Source: ..." on the left, `acp-logo-slate.svg` on the right. Logo width is about 9% of the chart width: 64px minimum, 96px maximum.
- One y-axis only. Two units make two charts.
- If the point is one number, show the number (Google Sans 600) with a dated comparison. Do not make a chart.

Categorical colours, in this fixed order. Never skip a colour.

| Order | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Royal | #2A58EF |
| 2 | Sun | #E5352A |
| 3 | Violet | #6C3F92 |
| 4 | Sky | #2F86D6 |
| 5 | Ochre | #C0621E |
| Other | Warm grey | #C9C4BC |

- One series is always Royal.
- More than five series: put the rest in "Other" (warm grey) or use small multiples.
- A company keeps the same colour in all charts of one document.
- Text never uses a series colour. Use ink #1A1A1A and grey #5F5A55.
- Amounts (heat maps): #DBE8FF, #B5CDFF, #89AFFF, #5C8BFF, #2A58EF, #2146BC, #16328A. Ink text on the first four, white text on the last three.
- Plus and minus: Royal #2A58EF for up, Sun #E5352A for down, #ECE8E2 for zero.
- This order passes colour-blind separation and 3:1 contrast on white and #FDF8F4.
- In HTML charts, add a hover tooltip and a data table.

## Final check

- Names written as in the table.
- Logo from `assets/logos/`, correct version for the ground, clear space kept.
- Lora for titles, Google Sans (or Noto Sans) for body.
- Colours from the tables only. No gradient in documents or on web pages.
- Charts follow the template and the colour order.
